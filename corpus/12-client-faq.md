# Client FAQ

*Fictional company. Answers to the questions our clients ask most often.*

## Where is my website hosted?

All client websites and data are hosted in France, at OVHcloud (Roubaix), with backups replicated at Scaleway (Paris). No client data is stored outside the European Union.

## How do I report a problem?

Open a ticket from your client area, email support@nexa-digital.example, or call 02 41 00 00 00 during business hours (Monday to Friday, 8 am to 7 pm). Please describe what you expected, what happened, and include a screenshot if possible.

## I forgot my client-area password. What should I do?

Click "Forgot password" on the login page. A reset link valid for **30 minutes** is sent to your email address. For security reasons our support team never asks for your password.

## Can I get a copy of an old invoice?

Yes. All invoices are available in the "Billing" section of your client area for 10 years.

## How do I cancel my maintenance contract?

Maintenance contracts renew automatically each year. To stop yours, send a registered letter at least three months before the renewal date.

## Do you offer training for our staff?

Yes, on-site or remote, for groups of up to 8 people. Topics include office tools, managing your website and cybersecurity awareness.

## Who owns the code of my website?

The code developed specifically for you becomes yours once the project is fully paid. Generic Nexa components remain ours and are licensed to you.
